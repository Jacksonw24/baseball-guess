    Who's the Ballplayer?

    Identify MLB players from their year-by-year stat line.

    How to play

    • A stat table loads with the player's name hidden. Type their name into the input — autocomplete suggests matches.
    • You get 6 guesses. Use the Hint button if you're stuck (each hint costs 2 points). Wins score max(10 − guesses − 2×hints, 1).
    • Three filters up top: Difficulty (Famous / Pros / All-time), Era, and Position. Pool size shown live.

    🚨 Extreme Mode 🚨

    • Win 5 in a row and the next round flips to Extreme — the whole UI turns red.
    • The player's name is revealed but the Team column is blanked. Type their teams in chronological order (e.g. Red Sox, then Dodgers).
    • 3 strikes (wrong / out-of-order / unknown team) and you're out. Clear it for +25 points and a 🚨 badge on the leaderboard.
    • Chain clears scale the difficulty: Level II/III/IV/V/VI add fewer strikes, harder players, hidden team count. Any loss resets the chain.

    Share + leaderboard

    • After any round, 📋 Copy share link sends a friend the exact player to try.
    • Wins post to a global leaderboard with your username and a 🚨×N badge for total Extremes cleared.

    Pick a username
